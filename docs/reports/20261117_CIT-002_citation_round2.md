## 1. How the statuses are defined

Identical to `CIT-001` §1: `verified` requires a literal extract from the cited source that contains the number, phrase or attribution the sentence attributes to that key; `partial` = the source was read and supports only part of it; `not-supported` = solid probing shows the attributed content absent or contradicted; `pending` = the source could not be consulted at all (method and error recorded). **No key is marked `verified` without a quoted extract.**
